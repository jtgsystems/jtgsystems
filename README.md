## 🏢 About JTG Systems (Joint Technology Group Inc.)

Headquartered in the Niagara Region of Ontario, **JTG Systems** is a premier technology firm and specialized hardware engineering laboratory. We deliver mission-critical support, custom computing hardware, and advanced technology solutions for consumers, professionals, and enterprise clients.

* **💻 Precision Hardware Lab**: Rapid-turnaround diagnostics, micro-soldering, board-level repairs, screen and power subsystem remediation for PCs, MacBooks, workstations, and mobile devices.
